A coaching loop that turns the gap between your best agents and the average into provably coachable training simulations — surfaced automatically from real escalation data.

The problem

When the bot hands a conversation to a human, some agents recover it (resolve on first contact) and some don't.

Where the good agents succeed and the average doesn't, that gap is a provably coachable skill — so it's a training opportunity, not just a bad-luck escalation.

Who it's for

A team lead who wants to act on the data, not just read it.

They need to know which escalation types are coachable right now, what the best agents actually do differently, and a one-click path to turn that into an interaction simulation they can assign.

The flow — built from the end backward
