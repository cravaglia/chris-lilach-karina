✦ What to put in the simulation

Best resolvers on special-handling exceptions win on Expectations and Context — the two widest gaps vs. the team. Build simulations where the agent inherits a customer who has already looped with the bot, and must: (1) open by acknowledging the prior friction, (2) state a concrete next step and timeframe before acting, and (3) use the carried-over history rather than re-asking. Score on whether they set expectations in the first turn and confirm the resolution will hold.
